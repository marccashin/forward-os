⚙️ Adjustment Settings Step 2
These per-unit dollar adjustments normalize each comp to the subject property. Positive (+) = comp is inferior to subject. Negative (–) = comp is superior.
GLA Adjustment
Per sq ft above grade. Applied when comp GLA differs from subject.
$/sqft
Below-Grade Adjustment
Per sq ft finished below grade (basement). Typically 40–60% of above-grade rate.
$/sqft
Bedroom Adjustment
Per bedroom difference. Applied when comp bedroom count differs from subject.
$/bed
Full Bath Adjustment
Per full bathroom difference between comp and subject property.
$/bath
Half Bath Adjustment
Per half bathroom difference. Typically 40–50% of full bath rate.
$/half bath
Garage Adjustment
Per garage space difference between comp and subject property.
$/space
HOA Adjustment (monthly)
Monthly HOA difference × this multiplier. Reflects capitalized cost to buyer.
× multiplier
